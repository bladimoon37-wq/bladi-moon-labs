The problem

Internet problems have a habit of disappearing when support arrives.

A quick speed test may look normal even when your connection drops intermittently. Internet Black Box is designed to stay running in the background and preserve what happened over time.

How it works

Start it. Leave it running. Review the evidence.

01

Start monitoring

Run Internet Black Box on your Windows PC and continue using your computer normally.

02

Connectivity is observed

The application records network observations and separates normal periods from detected connectivity anomalies.

03

Export the evidence

Generate a readable report and export an evidence package containing supporting diagnostic data.

Evidence

Built around observable facts.

Reports focus on when a connectivity anomaly was observed, how long it lasted, what monitoring layers responded, and the diagnostic evidence associated with the event.

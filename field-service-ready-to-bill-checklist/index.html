Completed Doesn't Mean Ready to Bill: A Field Service Billing Checklist

Your technician finished the work. That does not necessarily mean the job is ready for billing.

A completed field service job can still stall before invoicing if the customer's billing requirements are not satisfied. Check the job against the requirements for that specific customer and work order before the invoice goes out.

Ready-to-bill checklist

  • PO number: Is the required purchase order present and tied to the correct job?
  • Work order reference: Is the customer or platform work order number included?
  • Technician notes: Do the notes clearly describe the work performed and outcome?
  • Labor and parts: Are billable labor, materials, and parts supported by the job record?
  • Completion photos: Are required before/after or completion photos present?
  • Customer sign-off: Is the required signature or acknowledgement present?
  • Approvals: Were required quote, change, or exception approvals obtained?
  • NTE limit: Is the amount within the not-to-exceed limit, or is an exception documented?
  • Customer-specific requirements: Is there anything unique to this account, location, portal, or contract?

A simple manual preflight

Put the customer's requirements on one side and the completed job record on the other. Mark each requirement as present, missing, not applicable, or unclear. Do not guess missing references or approvals. Resolve the gaps first.

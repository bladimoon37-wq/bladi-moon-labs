What happened?

The local gateway became unreachable during the opening diagnostic cycle. External checks were unavailable at the same time. On the following diagnostic cycle, the gateway and external connectivity had recovered.

How long?

The recorded affected time in this QA example was 5.102 seconds.

What does that prove?

It proves only what the PC observed during that recorded event. Because the local gateway itself was unreachable, this example is not strong evidence of an upstream ISP-side fault.

Evidence boundary: Internet Black Box records observable connectivity conditions. It does not establish legal liability or automatically prove that an ISP, router, DNS provider, device, or another party caused an incident.

Why the distinction matters

An evidence recorder is more trustworthy when it preserves inconvenient observations too. IBB is designed to organize the observations first and avoid assigning a cause the evidence does not support.

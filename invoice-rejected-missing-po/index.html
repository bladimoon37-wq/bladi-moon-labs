Invoice Rejected Because of a Missing PO? What to Check Before Resubmitting

A missing purchase order can stop an otherwise completed job from moving through a customer's billing process.

Why a missing PO can block the invoice

Some customers require a valid PO or purchase-order reference before an invoice can be accepted. If that requirement applies, resubmitting without resolving it may simply repeat the delay.

Don't invent or reuse a PO

A PO is a customer-controlled reference. Do not guess a number, copy one from an unrelated job, or alter documentation just to get the invoice through. Confirm the correct reference from the customer, work order, approved system, or other authorized source.

Before you resubmit

  • Confirm whether a PO is required for this customer and job.
  • Verify the PO against the correct work order, location, or authorized customer record.
  • Check the work order reference.
  • Confirm technician notes and labor/parts documentation.
  • Confirm required completion photos.
  • Confirm customer signature or sign-off when required.
  • Confirm approvals and exception documentation.
  • Check the amount against the applicable NTE limit.
  • Recheck other customer-specific supporting documents.

Do not just fix the PO. Recheck the whole billing package so the next submission does not fail for a second missing requirement.
